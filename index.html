01 / THE REALITY

School already gives you enough to think about.

Keeping track of every assignment, exam, tuition slot and change of plan can become its own workload. StudyOS is built to take some of that organising off your plate.

A WORKSPACE THAT ADAPTS

Stop rebuilding your week every time something changes.

Put your commitments in one place, shape a realistic plan, then adjust it in conversation when real life gets in the way.

02 / THE TOOLKIT

Everything has a place. Your plan has a pulse.

StudyOS connects the moving parts of your academic week, from the first deadline you add to the focus session you finish.

01 / TALK IT THROUGH↗

An AI assistant that updates your plan

Tell the assistant about a new task, a changed deadline or a commitment in your week. It can update your planner for you, so you spend less time managing the system.

02 / MAKE IT REALISTIC◷

A study planner built around your time

Bring together your availability, deadlines, exams and regular activities to generate a schedule that reflects the time you actually have.

03 / KEEP THE IMPORTANT DATES CLOSE⌁

Deadlines and exam preparation

Track what is due, estimate the work involved and organise exam preparation around subjects, syllabus workload and revision needs.

04 / SHOW UP, THEN SEE IT◌

Focus sessions and progress

Use a dedicated focus timer, keep a record of sessions and see progress through the study blocks you complete.

05 / BUILD YOUR ROUTINE＋

Activities and availability

Add school, tuition, appointments, recurring commitments and time away. Your plan can account for the parts of life that happen outside studying.

06 / A GENTLE NUDGE◷

Reminders that fit your day

Set reminder times around your routine and use notifications to bring your next study moment back into view.

03 / THE FLOW

From scattered tasks to a plan you can work with.

You do not need to have the perfect week figured out before you begin. Start with what you know. Adjust as you go.

STEP 01

Tell StudyOS what is on

Add your deadlines, exams, available hours and regular commitments. You can also tell the AI assistant what has changed.

STEP 02

Build a workable schedule

Generate a study plan around the time and workload you have, then review the blocks it creates.

STEP 03

Keep adapting and moving

Update the plan when things shift, focus on a study block and use your progress to see what is getting done.

04 / YOUR WORKSPACE

A clearer view of what comes next.

Your StudyOS workspace brings the day into focus: the next study block, what is coming up and the progress you are making. The planning tools are there when you need to make a change.
